PROCEED TO THE HELICOPTER

Night · Rain · One way out

Your five-agent cell has been burned. The district is locked down and crawling with hostiles. A helicopter is holding on the far side of the city — get your team across the streets, through the alleys and over the rooftops, and onto that bird.

Tap a tile to preview · tap again to move · tap an enemy to aim · drag to pan · pinch to zoom · twist to rotate

FIELD MANUAL

  • Move: tap a tile to preview the route (blue = 1 action, yellow = dash), tap it again to go.
  • Shoot: tap a visible enemy, check the hit breakdown, press FIRE. You can also shoot cars, fuel barrels, pumps, hydrants and street lamps.
  • Stealth: red tiles are enemy vision cones. Lit areas get you spotted from further away — shoot out lamps to make darkness.
  • Witnesses: if an enemy spots you, kill them before your turn ends or the alarm brings reinforcements.
  • Accuracy: cover, smoke, darkness, height and field of view all matter. Hitting enemies outside their view (or unaware) is much easier.
  • Snipers paint a target with a red laser and fire next turn. Break line of sight or drop smoke.
  • Destruction: rockets and grenades flatten walls and cover. Cars explode when shot enough — and set off alarms that lure guards.
  • Camera: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to rotate (or use ↺ ↻). Tap the compass to face north again.
  • Extract: step an agent into the green landing zone and press EXTRACT.

Tap anywhere to close.

WEBGPU NEEDED

This game renders with WebGPU. On iPad use Safari on iPadOS 26 or later (or enable WebGPU under Settings → Safari → Advanced → Feature Flags on iPadOS 18). On desktop use a current Chrome, Edge or Safari.

GRAPHICS RESET

The system reset the graphics device, which can happen after switching apps. Reload to keep playing; the current mission restarts.
